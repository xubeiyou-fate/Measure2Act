# Code of conduct

## Expected conduct

Project participants must communicate respectfully, discuss technical and
scientific disagreements on their merits, acknowledge uncertainty, and avoid
harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or disclosure of another person's
private information.

Research claims must remain within the documented evidence boundary. Do not
present retrospective results as operational aviation validation, suppress
negative results, or upload restricted trajectories, credentials, private
links, or unpublished model assets to issues or pull requests.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews, and other project-managed communication spaces. Maintainers may edit,
hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may
restrict participation when necessary.

## Reporting

Report conduct involving security-sensitive or private material through the
private route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct concerns,
contact the maintainers privately rather than posting personal information in
a public issue. Reports should identify the relevant interaction and will be
reviewed as confidentially as practical.
